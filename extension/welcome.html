Welcome to Player Switch

Set up the shared display in three steps. Afterwards, a tap on a tile switches players.

  1. Pin the icon

    Chrome puts new extensions behind the puzzle piece next to the address bar. Click it, then the pin next to Player Switch for Autodarts, and the icon stays in the toolbar.

  2. Save the first player

    Open Autodarts and sign in. In the next ten minutes, the account is saved by itself, and the icon shows a check mark. Someone who is signed in already is saved the same way.

    Open Autodarts

  3. Add the next players

    Click the icon, choose Add account and sign in as the next player: that account is saved by itself too.

    A + on the icon means that someone is signed in who isn't saved yet: Save account in the popup saves them.

Everything stays on this computer and goes only to Autodarts.
