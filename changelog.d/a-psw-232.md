### Features

- In the venue mode, a player looks after their own account without the manager: while an account that was added at the display after the manager PIN plays and has no PIN, **Protect with a PIN** below its tile gives it one, and once it has a PIN, **Remove my account** removes it from the display with that PIN and signs it out at Autodarts. So a guest who adds their account while the manager isn't there no longer leaves a session on the display for anyone to play under. The accounts that the manager keeps, those saved before the manager PIN and the house account, and a PIN that an account has, still change only with the manager PIN.
