### Features

- In the venue mode, a player looks after their own account without the manager: while their account plays and has no PIN, **Protect with a PIN** below its tile gives it one, and once it has a PIN, **Remove my account** removes it from the display with that PIN and signs it out at Autodarts. So a guest who adds their account while the manager isn't there no longer leaves a session on the display for anyone to play under. A PIN that an account has, and the PIN of the house account, still change only with the manager PIN.
