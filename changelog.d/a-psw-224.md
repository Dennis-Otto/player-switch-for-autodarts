### Bug fixes

- With **Return after a break** in the venue mode, a lobby that waits for an opponent, or for the rest of a group, no longer loses the display after the set minutes without a tap: a lobby holds the display for three times the break, so the players can join without touching the screen, and a lobby that everyone walked away from still returns the display in the end. The list of the open lobbies and the page that sets up a new one count as a break, as before.
