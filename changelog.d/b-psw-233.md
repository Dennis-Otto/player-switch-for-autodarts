### Features

- **Copy diagnostics** in **Manage accounts**, beside **Report a problem**, puts what a bug report needs on the clipboard: the versions, the browser and its language, how each saved account stands, by its number and never its name, which settings and locks of the venue mode are on, and the last 50 events of the extension, such as a switch, a join or a renewal and each request to Autodarts that failed, with its time, how it ended and the HTTP status. It holds no name, username, token or password; the field *Logs* of the bug form and the help say where it goes, and **Forget all accounts** deletes the events too.
- A display with many accounts finds a player quickly: from nine saved accounts, a field above the tiles finds one by the name or the username as you type, and on the Autodarts page the letters go into it by themselves, so the keys 1 to 9 then pick among the tiles that show and every player is a key away. **Settings** now puts the tiles **By name**, the name that a tile shows first, or **Last played first**, so that the regulars of a club find themselves at the top. **By name** now follows the name on the tile instead of the username, so after the update the tiles and their keys 1 to 9 may move once.
