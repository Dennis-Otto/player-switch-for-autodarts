### Features

- Each time Player Switch saves an account, by **Add account** or by **Save account**, its message reminds you to share the board: it names the Autodarts username of the account, with which the owner of the board shares it under **My Boards** on Autodarts, and links to the help for a player who sees no board. Until now only the welcome page of the install said so, and an account added later got no reminder.
